The Aerojet M1 was the most powerful liquid hydrogen rocket engine ever designed by the United States, yet it remains one of the greatest forgotten masterpieces in aerospace history. Designed to propel humanity far beyond the moon, this massive propulsion system was defeated not by engineering flaws, but by shifting political priorities and the fading ambition of the American space program.

In this documentary, we explore the hidden history behind the Aerojet (/m/01c90_) M1 and its intended role in the post-Apollo (/m/01w5s) era. You will learn the profound technical challenges of scaling liquid hydrogen engines, how the M1 compared to the legendary Rocketdyne F-1 (/m/02xg4x) that powered the Saturn V (/m/06x4_), and the crucial lessons this abandoned project reveals about the intersection of technological capability and government funding. By examining the context of the 1960s space race driven by NASA (/m/05f4p), we uncover what humanity loses when revolutionary projects are left behind.
